# Tillandsia, the product

Tillandsia is a simple-to-use, simple-to-customize tabletop and board game simulator to be shared with friends online.
its unique selling points are:

- **Simple authoring and customization**: The engine is designed from the ground up to be scriptable with an easy-to-learn programming
  language, LUA, and exposes the primitives needed to build many types of games without being overly restrictive or tedious to use.
- **Designed for game designers as well as players**: Tillandsia is, first and foremost, social: it should exist as a marketplace to
  allow authors of new games to prototype, iterate on ideas, or even publish full games, not just simulate existing ones.
- **Simple subscription model**: Access to one table is free; no pay walls, no subscription, no nothing. Just an account. Unlimited
  tables cost $5/month, Authoring/GM tools cost $5/month, no other fees. All features are available on all tables regardless of paid or
  free.
- **Extensive GM/DM tools**: As well as being a great place to play games for players, GMs should have a suite of tools that makes
  their lives easier to help automate and simplify the boring parts of development.

The goal is simple: be a better Roll20 at a lower cost, and help aspiring game designers build new games and play them with their'
friends.
